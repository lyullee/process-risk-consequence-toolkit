# Contributing

Contributions must be original or demonstrably licensed for inclusion under
the project's terms. Include provenance and compatible notices for third-party
expression/data. Do not contribute extracts from a purchased standard or book.
You retain your copyright while licensing your contributions under Apache-2.0.

For a model addition, state equations, SI contracts, assumptions, unsupported
cases, uncertainty/validation limits and primary references. Add meaningful
analytic/conservation/refinement tests and negative gates. Numerical agreement
with your own implementation alone is not independent validation.

Generate schemas/manuals, run tests/build/archive audit, and record evidence.
Do not introduce credentials, source data downloads or network/optional engine
execution into base imports. See docs/DEVELOPMENT.md.
